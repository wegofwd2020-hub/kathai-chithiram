## Milestones

- **M2 · Multi-user program platform (family / child / therapist + programs + reporting)** — the shift from the single-operator CLI *engine* to a *platform*: a guided story-authoring template (part a), a people/family/child domain with account-scoped access behind the ADR-004 `IdentityProvider` seam (part b), and therapist-established programs with parent-facing progress reports (part c). **Shaping ADR: `docs/ADR_005_multi_user_program_platform.md` (Proposed).** Sequenced by data risk: the story template adds no new personal data and is buildable now; **accounts + child DOB materially expand special-category processing and contradict the current minimization stance**, so they are gated on a DPIA revision (ADR-005 Decision 7 — DPIA/notice/consent/retention/DPO), mirroring the M1 engine gate. Data-minimization decisions taken up front: capture the *child's* DOB only (parent/therapist DOB dropped), prefer the least-granular age form that serves progress-norming. NEXT (ungated): build the story template (ADR-005 D6); ratify the domain shape; then, post-gate, accounts (grants lifted to `child_id`) → programs + reports.

- **M1 · Per-child progress quantification → therapist-suggested premise customization** — quantify each child's feedback (part c) into a progress measure (e.g. % independent over last K sessions, mastery flag, regulation trend) that may *suggest* — never auto-author — an updated premise (part a) for future stories. The therapist decides; the safety gate still runs (ADR-001 Decision 5). **Stance ADR: `docs/ADR_002_progress_quantification_premise_suggestion.md` (Accepted)** — capture-first, suggestion-only, engine deferred behind preconditions; the capture-track primitive schema has landed. **Engine-design ADR: `docs/ADR_003_progress_engine_design.md`** — a deterministic, policy-driven measure + suggestion seam where the clinical parameters (window K, thresholds, trends) are collaborator-authored `ProgressPolicy` configuration, never engineer-chosen constants. *Feedback primitives (`prompt_level` refused/prompted/independent, `completed`, `mood_checkin`) keyed to a structured `goal` id already accrue; the evidence view and accept/edit/dismiss path exist and compute no measure.* **Engine mechanics now built and merged** (`progress/policy.py` — the `ProgressPolicy`/`Metric`/`Condition`/`ThresholdRule` schema; `progress/engine.py` — the pure `measure`/`suggest` interpreter), shipping **no thresholds and no defaults** so nothing runs until a policy is supplied — which satisfies ADR-003's own Proposed→Accepted flip condition (mechanics landed default-free + referenced here). **Still gated off**: *enabling* the engine (loading a real collaborator-authored policy + wiring suggestions to `record_suggestion`) stays blocked on ADR-002 Decision 7's non-engineering preconditions — 7.1 policy authorship (see `docs/M1_PROFESSIONAL_COLLABORATOR_BRIEF.md` v0.2), 7.4 clinical-language review, 7.6 DPIA progress-profiling touchpoint.

- **M3 · Domain model — from a rented frontier model to an owned, clinically grounded one** — the shift from prompting a general model to owning the generation capability. **Shaping ADRs: `docs/ADR_006_domain_model_strategy.md` (base-model strategy — capability layers before weights, permissive-licence bar, evaluation as the acceptance gate), `docs/ADR_007_scene_script_v2_authoring_grammar.md` (a closed art vocabulary and a machine-checkable authoring grammar), `docs/ADR_008_training_corpus_provenance.md` (wholly synthetic, clinician-adjudicated corpus). Phased plan with resourcing and costs: `docs/LLM_PROGRAM_PLAN.md`.** The organising insight is that "understands special-needs story authoring" decomposes into five capabilities and only one of them lives in weights: structural conformance is a *decoder constraint*, renderable-art grounding is a *contract* problem, narrative grammar is mostly *validators*, situational judgement is the learned part, and risk-of-harm recognition is deliberately **not** a model decision (ADR-001 D5). So the sequence is contract → constraint → validators → corpus → adapter, and the first three need no ML, no GPU and no collaborator sign-off to begin — while improving the Anthropic path that runs today. **Driving reason is sovereignty, not cost:** `docs/DPIA.md` R2 currently rests on an operational precondition the client cannot verify (KC-6: ZDR is an org-level property of the credential); in-process generation removes the processor entirely and makes the claim verifiable by construction. Cost follows at volume, and one cost lever — caching the schema prompt prefix, re-serialised in full on every attempt today — is worth taking immediately and independently. **NEXT (ungated):** `KC-13` (art-vocabulary registry, first — today an unrenderable `setting` or `prop` silently degrades to a blank calm room and nothing reports it), then `KC-12` (constrained decoding + prompt caching). **Gated:** `KC-16`'s policy authorship, `KC-15` (corpus) and `KC-14`'s clinical preference axis all need a retained professional collaborator — the same engagement M1 already owes under ADR-002 D7.1/D7.4, and it should be **one** engagement covering both tracks. `KC-17` (local provider + LoRA adapter) opens only when `KC-14` is green with a recorded baseline. **Hard prohibitions carried forward:** no real child story is ever training data; `feedback.jsonl` never becomes a reward signal (ADR-002 D8's closed loop in its least inspectable form); no model-mediated harm triage; the KC-7 human review gate does not relax; no generative video (the renderer's determinism is what makes `guard_render` a guarantee rather than a sample).
